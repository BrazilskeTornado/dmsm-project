The formula

Hydration is the base layer of performance.

AuraPerform is designed around a simple idea: support fluid balance first, then add ingredients that help you keep producing quality work.

✦One serving15.6 g active formula · 500 ml water · zero added sugar

Why the combination works

Two systems. One training window.

Sweat changes more than your water balance. AuraPerform brings fluid-supporting ions and a well-studied performance ingredient together in one practical ritual.

01

Ionic hydration

Sodium is the lead electrolyte lost in sweat, while potassium, magnesium, and calcium complement normal fluid, muscle, and nerve function. The goal is a drink that is easy to sip consistently, not a heavy post-workout shake.

02

Daily performance base

Creatine monohydrate supports the phosphocreatine system used for short, repeated bursts of high-intensity effort. Beta-alanine is included for carnosine synthesis; its benefit comes from consistent daily use, not a single scoop.
