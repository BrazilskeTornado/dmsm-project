Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

How much should I take?

Mix one level scoop with 500 ml of cold water. Start with one serving per day and follow the product label or advice from your qualified healthcare professional.

Do I need to take it only during workouts?

No. Creatine is most useful as a consistent daily habit. The intra-workout format is a convenient way to remember it, but timing is less important than regular use.

Does it replace a meal or recovery shake?

No. AuraPerform is a hydration and performance drink mix, not a complete source of calories, protein, or carbohydrates.

Is it suitable for everyone?

Adults should read the label and consider individual needs. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, or managing a health condition, consult a healthcare professional before use.

How should I store it?

Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry place. Use the included scoop and keep the powder away from moisture.
